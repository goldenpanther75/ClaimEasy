Key Points to Remember

Before buying your health insurance policy

Sum Insured

Choose a cover that is sufficient for your family's needs, considering medical inflation.

Network Hospitals

Check the list of cashless network hospitals near your residence.

Waiting Period

Understand the waiting period for pre-existing diseases (usually 2-4 years).

Co-payment

Check if there is any co-payment clause where you share a part of the claim amount.

Claim Settlement Ratio

High CSR indicates reliable claim settlement by the insurer.

OPD Coverage

Verify if the plan covers Out-Patient Department expenses like consultations.
