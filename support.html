Frequently Asked Questions

How do I file a cashless claim?

To file a cashless claim, visit the network hospital and present your ClaimEasy health card. The hospital will contact our TPA to authorize the treatment. Once approved, the bills are settled directly.

Can I cancel my policy and get a refund?

Yes, you can cancel your policy within the free-look period (usually 15 days from receipt of policy documents). A pro-rata premium will be refunded after deducting stamp duty and medical/risk charges.

What is a No Claim Bonus (NCB)?

NCB is a reward for not making any claim in the preceding years. It can be used to get a discount on the renewal premium or to increase the sum insured amount, depending on the product.

How can I add a family member to my health policy?

You can usually add a family member (spouse or child) during the policy renewal. In case of marriage or childbirth, some policies allow mid-term inclusion. Please update your details using the 'Update Details' section.

How do I track my claim status?

You can track your claim status by visiting the Claim Assistance page. Enter your claim number or policy number to see real-time updates.

How can I download my policy copy?

Log in to your account, go to the 'My Policies' section, and click on the 'Download' icon next to your active policy. You can also request a copy via WhatsApp.

What documents are needed for a car insurance claim?

Typically, you need the Policy Copy, Registration Certificate (RC), Driving License (DL) of the driver, and a filled Claim Form. For accidents, an FIR might be required.
